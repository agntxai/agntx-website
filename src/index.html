The Lapis Story

Organizations keep losing what they know.

Every company we meet has the same hidden problem: its most valuable knowledge — how things actually work, why decisions were made, where the risks really sit — lives in a few people's heads and a pile of stale documents. When those people leave, or a new team arrives, the organization starts over. Again.

Our founder spent more than 30 years inside some of the world's largest technology companies, leading modernization programs and inventing the methods teams still use to untangle complex systems. The methods worked. The understanding still evaporated.

Lapis is the answer he wanted for his whole career: an AI-native engine that captures how your business really runs, keeps it current, and uses it to build and run systems. Knowledge compounds instead of resetting.

The question isn't “how do we go faster?” It's “how do we stop starting over?”

Claims processing

A national fleet-claims processor

Its core claims intake ran on tooling deprecated in 2007, took six weeks to build, and had been built twice by two teams. Neither version could onboard a new client. Lapis reimagined it as one provider-agnostic framework in six hours. The next client is a configuration change, not a project.

Enterprise integration

No user stories, no analysis, no architecture — just a PDF and one kickoff meeting. Lapis turned the meeting into project memory, derived the data mapping, documented every design decision, and delivered a working multi-service integration in about two days.
